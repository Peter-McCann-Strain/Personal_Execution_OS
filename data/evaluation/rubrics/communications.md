# Communications Rubric v2

This rubric extends [universal.md](universal.md). Use it to judge whether the
communications specialist drafts messages that fit the relationship, purpose,
and requested level of polish. Universal dimensions remain on the 5-point
Likert scale; specialist dimensions below use binary yes/no sub-checks.

## Binary Scoring

Each sub-check (Q1, Q2, ...) is scored Y (1) or N (0). A dimension's score is
the sum of Ys across its sub-checks. Thresholds for pass/fail gates are
configured in settings. Binary rubrics were adopted after April 2026 evidence
(RULERS, arXiv:2601.08654) that yes/no prompts yield the highest inter-rater
reliability for LLM judges.

## Specialist Dimension 1: Voice Match

- Q1: Does the draft match the requested tone (formal / casual / blunt / warm)? Y/N
- Q2: Does the register fit the relationship (colleague, client, friend, stranger)? Y/N
- Q3: Is the draft appropriate for the channel (email / chat / message)? Y/N
- Q4: Does the draft preserve the user's intent without reshaping it? Y/N

## Specialist Dimension 2: Professional Quality

- Q1: Is the draft clear and well-structured (no organisational issues)? Y/N
- Q2: Does it include the essential context or requested logistics? Y/N
- Q3: Is it ready to send with minimal or no edits? Y/N
- Q4: Does it avoid over-writing a simple communication (right-sized)? Y/N

## Anti-Pattern Checklist

- Anti-Pattern: sounding robotic when the user asked for something human.
- Anti-Pattern: changing the user's intent instead of expressing it clearly.
- Anti-Pattern: omitting essential context or requested logistics.
- Anti-Pattern: over-writing a simple communication request.
