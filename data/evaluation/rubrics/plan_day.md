# Plan Day Rubric v2

This rubric extends [universal.md](universal.md). It focuses on whether the
day-planning specialist turns goals and commitments into a realistic schedule
that the user can actually follow. Universal dimensions remain on the 5-point
Likert scale; specialist dimensions below use binary yes/no sub-checks.

## Binary Scoring

Each sub-check (Q1, Q2, ...) is scored Y (1) or N (0). A dimension's score is
the sum of Ys across its sub-checks. Thresholds for pass/fail gates are
configured in settings. Binary rubrics were adopted after April 2026 evidence
(RULERS, arXiv:2601.08654) that yes/no prompts yield the highest inter-rater
reliability for LLM judges.

## Specialist Dimension 1: Daily Feasibility

- Q1: Does the plan fit the hours available in the day (no overpacking)? Y/N
- Q2: Does it respect existing calendar commitments and deadlines? Y/N
- Q3: Does it include buffers or transitions between blocks? Y/N
- Q4: Does it account for energy constraints (e.g. deep-work before fatigue)? Y/N

## Specialist Dimension 2: Focus Alignment

- Q1: Are the most important outcomes placed in prime / deep-work time? Y/N
- Q2: Is low-value busywork kept out of protected blocks? Y/N
- Q3: Does the plan surface a single top priority for the day? Y/N
- Q4: Does the output read as an actual plan (time-blocked) rather than a flat list? Y/N

## Anti-Pattern Checklist

- Anti-Pattern: filling the day with low-value chores.
- Anti-Pattern: ignoring deadlines, meetings, or urgent carry-over work.
- Anti-Pattern: over-scheduling without buffers or transitions.
- Anti-Pattern: presenting a list rather than an actual plan.
